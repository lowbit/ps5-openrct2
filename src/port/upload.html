Send RollerCoaster Tycoon 2 to OpenRCT2

OpenRCT2 needs the game files of your copy of RollerCoaster Tycoon 2: the folder with Data, ObjData, Scenarios, Tracks and Landscapes in it. Choose or drop that folder, or a ZIP file of it. Other files in it are left out.

The console is not listening. Start OpenRCT2 on the PS5; it waits for the game files on its first start.
